# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique code, not the person who
wrote it.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback that is specific and actionable.
- Accept feedback the same way you would want it given.
- Respect that contributors have different levels of experience, and that
  everyone was a beginner at some point.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing someone's
private information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer directly.
Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues,
or block accounts that violate this policy.

This document is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
